Helpful Tips

  • Keep a steady sleep schedule to support your mood and energy.
  • Take 5 to 10 minutes a day for breathing or mindfulness.
  • Stay connected with one trusted person when you feel overwhelmed.
  • Small movement such as walking or stretching can improve focus and calm.

When to Seek Extra Support

If your worries or sadness are lasting for days, affecting everyday life, or making you feel unsafe, reach out to a trusted professional or local support service.
