Development of an AI-Powered Mental Well-being Assessment Website for Senior High School Students

A research study summary based on the document uploaded in the project folder.

Researchers: Padilla, Micha Mariella S.; Nening, Nicole M.; Cataga, Christian Jay A.; Quirante, Mark Raven D.; Morales, Charles Willy R.; Ecalner, Jonathan Jhay H.; Belolo, Manuel B.

Year: July 2026

Background of the Study

Mental well-being is a major factor in a student’s academic performance and overall quality of life. Senior high school students often face stress, anxiety, and emotional challenges caused by academic demands, family responsibilities, and social relationships.

The World Health Organization has emphasized that mental health conditions are common among adolescents, which shows the need for early awareness and support. Many students hesitate to seek help because of stigma or limited access to services. This study explores how an AI-powered website can provide a private, easy-to-access, and personalized mental well-being assessment.

Statement of the Problem

  • What is the level of effectiveness of the AI-powered mental well-being assessment website in promoting mental health awareness among senior high school students?
  • How accurate and appropriate are the AI-generated feedback and recommendations based on the users’ assessment responses?
  • Is there a significant relationship between the usability of the website and its effectiveness in promoting mental health awareness?

Objectives of the Study

  • To determine the level of usability of the AI-powered mental wellness assessment website in terms of ease of use, accessibility, and user satisfaction.
  • To determine the level of effectiveness of the website in promoting mental health awareness among senior high school students.
  • To evaluate the accuracy and appropriateness of the AI-generated feedback and recommendations.

Significance of the Study

This study is important for senior high school students, parents, web developers, AI engineers, and future researchers. It offers a practical example of how digital tools can support early mental health awareness and encourage timely support.

Why This Matters

The MindWell website reflects the same goal: to help users understand their emotional well-being, receive supportive guidance, and feel encouraged to seek help when needed.
