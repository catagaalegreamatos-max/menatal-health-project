    When to Seek Professional Help

    Consider speaking with a mental health professional if you:

    • Experience persistent sadness, anxiety, or stress for more than 2 weeks
    • Have difficulty functioning in daily activities (work, school, relationships)
    • Have thoughts of self-harm or suicide
    • Feel overwhelmed and don't know where to start
    • Need guidance on specific mental health concerns

    Crisis Resources: National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988 | Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
